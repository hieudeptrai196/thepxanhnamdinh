# Design System: Thép Xanh Nam Định FC

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

**Art Direction:** MATTE SPORTS EDITORIAL + RAW STADIUM ENERGY + THÀNH NAM IDENTITY

The visual language is a professional football club publication — grounded, physical, locally rooted, and unapologetically about football. The interface has the weight of a printed matchday programme, the urgency of a stadium scoreboard, and the gravitas of a sports documentary title sequence. Surfaces are matte and opaque. Typography carries the identity. Photography creates the drama — never gradients, never glow, never glass.

This is a website for 25,000 supporters who fill Thiên Trường every matchday. It should feel like something printed on thick stock and pinned to a dressing room wall — not something generated by an AI and posted on Dribbble.

**Atmosphere Calibration:**
- Creativity: 5/10 — expressive but disciplined. Never experimental for its own sake
- Visual Density: 6/10 — information-rich like a sports publication, never sparse like a luxury brand
- Roundness: 2/10 — sharp, architectural, stadium-built. Corners are cut, not melted
- Elevation: 1/10 — flat surfaces, no floating layers, no fake depth. Content sits ON the page, not above it
- Animation: 3/10 — restrained, mechanical, stadium-gate weight. No springy bouncing
- Decorative Effects: 2/10 — texture over effect, grain over glow, nothing for its own sake
- Variance: 5/10 — editorial asymmetry where it serves hierarchy, not random artsy chaos

**The site should feel like:**
- Official football club website (Celtic FC, Athletic Bilbao, Borussia Dortmund quality tier)
- Football magazine editorial (FourFourTwo, So Foot, Panenka)
- Matchday programme cover and interior
- Stadium concourse signage and wayfinding
- Sports documentary title sequence
- Thành Nam supporter culture — flags, tifo, chanting

**It must NEVER feel like:**
- AI-generated landing page or concept
- SaaS product or fintech dashboard
- Gaming UI / esports HUD
- Luxury tech website
- Generic Dribbble or Behance concept
- Cyberpunk or futuristic interface
- Trading card or collectible app
- E-commerce storefront

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Primary Palette

- **Club Blue** (`#009DDA`) — The matte blue of the club jersey. Primary buttons, active navigation, selected tabs, key links, live match accents. Use with restraint — Club Blue is strongest when it appears as punctuation against dark navy or white, not as a background flood. Never push brighter or add glow. This is a matte jersey color, not a neon light
- **Dark Navy** (`#071C2C`) — The stadium at night. Header, footer, match center, hero overlays, dark content sections. The primary dark surface — use instead of pure black everywhere. Solid and opaque, never transparent or blurred
- **Charcoal** (`#17232C`) — Secondary dark tone for card backgrounds on dark sections, subtle dark variation, secondary text weight when navy is too heavy. Slightly warmer than navy
- **Off White** (`#F3F3EF`) — The paper stock color. Primary light background. Warm enough to avoid clinical coldness, neutral enough to let photography breathe. This is NOT pure white — use it for page backgrounds and light sections
- **Light Gray** (`#E7EBEC`) — Structural role only. Table row alternation, section dividers, disabled states, card borders, background variation from off-white. Never used for text
- **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF`) — Card surfaces sitting on off-white backgrounds, clean reading containers, content areas that need maximum contrast

### Accent — Championship Gold

- **Championship Gold** (`#C3A044`) — SEVERELY RESTRICTED. Reserved exclusively for: the 3 championship stars on the crest, trophy iconography, championship year labels (`1965`, `1984`, `1985`), and heritage section micro-highlights. Maximum 2–3 gold elements per page. Never used for buttons, section backgrounds, headings, dividers, or decorative elements. The site is blue and navy, not gold. Gold is earned — it marks history, not decoration

### Functional Colors

- **Live Red** (`#D42B2B`) — Live match indicator dot, urgent alerts only. Matte, not neon. Small surface area
- **Win Green** (`#2D8A4E`) — Match won states, positive stat indicators. Muted forest tone, never bright
- **Draw Gray** (`#8C9196`) — Draw result states, neutral metadata
- **Body Text** (`#1A2530`) — Primary reading text. Deep near-black with subtle blue undertone. Grounded, not floating
- **Secondary Text** (`#5C6970`) — Metadata, captions, dates, bylines, secondary labels
- **Disabled Text** (`#9CA5AB`) — Placeholder text, inactive tab labels, disabled form elements

### Banned Colors & Effects

- Pure black (`#000000`) — BANNED. Use Dark Navy or Charcoal
- Neon blue / electric blue (anything brighter than `#009DDA`) — BANNED
- Purple in any context — BANNED
- Orange or warm accents — BANNED
- Any gradient as a surface fill — BANNED. Use solid flat colors
- Blue-to-purple or blue-to-cyan gradients — BANNED
- Any color at saturation above 85% — BANNED (except Club Blue itself)
- Colored shadows — BANNED. Shadows are neutral only
- Neon glow of any color — BANNED

## 3. Typography Rules

Typography carries the identity of this site. The typefaces must feel like stadium signage and sports publication mastheads — condensed, authoritative, physical.

### Display / Headlines — Bebas Neue

**Primary Display:** `Bebas Neue` — The stadium voice. All-caps condensed for hero headlines, section titles, matchday headers, large scores, year numbers, jersey numbers. Track at `0.02em` to prevent cramping at display sizes, `0.05em` at small label sizes.

Use Bebas Neue for text that should feel like it was painted on a stadium wall or printed on a programme cover. Set large — `clamp(2rem, 6vw, 4.5rem)` for heroes, `clamp(1.5rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)` for section headers.

### Secondary Display / Headings — Barlow

**Subheadings/UI Headers:** `Barlow 700–900` — Mixed-case or uppercase for subheadings, player names, card titles, navigation labels, tab headers. Industrial, sporty, excellent Vietnamese support. The workhorse display face.

### Large Numbers as Graphic Elements

Numbers are visual design elements, not just data. Jersey numbers, scores, years, and statistics should be set in `Bebas Neue` at oversized scale (2–5× body size) and used compositionally:
- `03` — player number partially behind a player photo
- `09` — jersey number as background graphic at 20% opacity
- `1965` — championship year as a section anchor
- `25.000` — attendance figure dominating a stat block

Numbers may cross grid boundaries when the composition justifies it. They should feel stamped, not typed.

### Body — Barlow

**Body Text:** `Barlow 400` — Industrial sans-serif with full Vietnamese support. Clean, readable, and consistent with heading font for unified visual identity. Test with: Thép Xanh Nam Định, Thiên Trường, Phổ Minh.

- Line height: `1.65`
- Max reading width: `65ch`
- Minimum size: `1rem` / `16px`
- Color: `#1A2530`

**Body Bold:** `Barlow 600` — Inline emphasis, pull quotes, strong labels. Never use 800+ for body context.

**Body Small:** `Barlow 400` at `0.875rem` / `14px` — Metadata, dates, captions, table cells.

### Monospace — JetBrains Mono

`JetBrains Mono 500` for match times, live scores, countdown timers, statistics tables, ticket codes, fixture times. At body size or smaller. The scoreboard typeface.

### Typography Enforcement

- Uppercase is reserved for: Bebas Neue display headlines, navigation items, category labels, match status badges. NEVER make body text, descriptions, or long sentences uppercase
- Heading hierarchy through weight AND size, never through color alone
- No decorative text shadows, text strokes, text outlines, or gradient text fills
- No text glow or neon text effects under any circumstance
- Pull quotes: `Archivo Condensed 600 Italic` at `1.5rem`, left-bordered with `3px` solid Club Blue line
- Letter spacing on Bebas: `0.02em` display, `0.05em` labels

### Banned Typography

- Inter — BANNED (generic, overused, screams "AI-generated")
- Poppins — BANNED (default AI template font)
- Generic serif fonts (Times, Georgia, Garamond, Palatino) — BANNED
- Decorative, script, or handwritten fonts — BANNED
- Thin/light weight display text (100–300 weight at large sizes) — BANNED. Display text must be heavy
- Gradient text on headers — BANNED
- Text shadow or text glow — BANNED

## 4. Component Stylings

### Buttons

Buttons are flat, solid, and direct — like a matchday ticket counter, not a luxury checkout.

- **Primary:** Solid `#009DDA` fill, white text, `Be Vietnam Pro 600` uppercase at `0.875rem`, `6px` radius, ZERO shadow, ZERO gradient. Hover: darken to `#0088BF`. Active: `translateY(-1px)`, darken to `#007AA8`. Transition `150ms`
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, `1px` solid `#009DDA` border, Club Blue text. Hover: `#009DDA` fill at `8%` opacity background. Same radius and text treatment
- **Dark variant:** Solid `#071C2C` fill, white text. For light backgrounds where blue would compete
- **Ghost (dark backgrounds):** Transparent fill, `1px` solid `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)` border, white text. Hover: border to `rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`
- **Disabled:** `#E7EBEC` fill, `#9CA5AB` text, no hover state, no pointer cursor

**Button bans:** No gradient fills. No glow. No glass. No pill shape (`40px+` radius). No shadows. No icons-only without text labels (except close/menu/back). No animated borders. Minimum touch target `44px` height.

### Cards — USE SPARINGLY

Cards are NOT the default container. This is the most important layout rule. Most content uses editorial composition: image + text side by side, thin horizontal dividers, background color blocks, grid structure with whitespace. A card should only appear when a content block must visually separate from identical siblings (e.g., one match preview among several).

**Instead of cards, prefer:**
- Horizontal rules (`1px` in `#E7EBEC` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) between content blocks
- Background color alternation (Off White / White, or Navy / Charcoal)
- Typographic hierarchy and spacing to create visual separation
- Full-bleed image + text editorial layouts

**When a card is genuinely necessary:**
- Radius: `6px` — crisp, not bubbly
- Border: `1px solid #E7EBEC` on light, `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark
- Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` MAXIMUM — barely visible. Zero shadow preferred
- Background: `#FFFFFF` on off-white, `#17232C` on dark navy
- No inner glow, no outer glow, no glassmorphism, no backdrop-blur, no gradient fill
- Hover: border darken slightly OR subtle `translateY(-1px)`. No scale, no shadow expansion, no glow

### Inputs / Forms

- Label above input in `Be Vietnam Pro 600` at `0.875rem`, `#1A2530`
- Input field: `1px solid #E7EBEC` border, `6px` radius, `#FFFFFF` fill, `16px` horizontal padding, `12px` vertical
- Focus: border changes to `#009DDA`, ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(0,157,218,0.15)`. No glow
- Error: border `#D42B2B`, message below in `#D42B2B` at `0.8rem`
- No floating labels. No animated underline inputs. No glass inputs

### Tables / Data Rows

Match fixtures, standings, and statistics use clean tabular or list-row layouts:
- Alternating row backgrounds: `#F3F3EF` / `#FFFFFF`
- `1px` horizontal dividers in `#E7EBEC`. No vertical borders
- Team names: `Archivo Condensed 700`. Scores: `Bebas Neue`. Metadata: `Be Vietnam Pro 400` small
- Tables must scroll horizontally inside their own container on mobile — never force body scroll

### Navigation

- **Desktop header:** Dark Navy `#071C2C` solid background, white text, `Archivo Condensed 600` uppercase. Active: white text with `2px` bottom border in Club Blue. Logo left. No glass, no blur, no transparency, no gradient
- **Mobile header:** Same solid dark navy, hamburger icon, slide-out or full-screen overlay. Simple slide transition, nothing elaborate
- **Tabs/category nav:** Horizontal row in `Archivo Condensed 600`. Active: Club Blue text + `2px` bottom border. Inactive: `#5C6970`. No pills, no filled tab backgrounds. On mobile: horizontal scroll or dropdown

### Badges / Status Tags

- `Bebas Neue` or `Archivo Condensed 700` at `0.75rem`, uppercase, `4px` radius, solid fill
- **LIVE:** `#D42B2B` fill, white text, optional small pulsing dot (the ONLY permitted perpetual animation)
- **FT:** `#071C2C` fill, white text
- **UPCOMING:** `#E7EBEC` fill, `#1A2530` text
- **Category tags:** `#F3F3EF` fill, `#17232C` text, `4px` radius, `1px` border `#E7EBEC`

### Loading States

- Skeleton loaders matching exact layout dimensions, `#E7EBEC` base with subtle left-to-right shimmer
- No circular spinners in-page
- The custom loading screen (Section 9) is a separate full-screen experience

## 5. Layout Principles

### Grid System

- Max content width: `1400px`, centered with auto margins
- Primary grid: 12-column, `24px` gutter desktop, `16px` mobile
- Section vertical spacing: `80px` desktop, `48px` mobile
- Internal spacing: `8px` base unit — `8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80`

### Editorial Composition — THE CORE LAYOUT PHILOSOPHY

This is a sports editorial site, not a SaaS dashboard. Layout should reference football magazine and matchday programme composition:

- **Asymmetric splits:** Large image (7–8 columns) beside narrow text column (4–5 columns). Use for features, player spotlights, stadium sections. INSTEAD OF centered hero with text-over-image, use left-heavy image + right text column
- **Oversized numbers as graphic elements:** Jersey numbers, years, attendance figures set at `8–12rem` in Bebas Neue, allowed to cross column boundaries, sit partially behind imagery, or anchor a section. These are compositional elements, not just data
- **Strong horizontal rules:** `1px` or `2px` lines in `#E7EBEC` (light) or `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)` (dark). Use INSTEAD of cards to separate content blocks
- **Varied content blocks:** News uses mixed sizes — one large feature (7 columns) + smaller items (5 columns). NEVER use 3 identical cards in a row
- **Full-bleed photography:** Key images span full viewport width. Text overlaid only with Dark Navy gradient overlay at `70–85%` opacity, bottom-up, for readability
- **Background color sections:** Alternate Off White and Dark Navy full-width blocks. This creates visual rhythm WITHOUT cards

### Anti-Layout Patterns

- 3-column equal card grids — BANNED. Vary sizes: 2+1, 1+2, editorial stagger, or mixed grid
- Every content block wrapped in a rounded rectangle — BANNED. Most content is unwrapped
- Centered hero with text + subtitle + 2 buttons stacked — BANNED when asymmetric composition would be stronger
- Flexbox percentage math / `calc()` hacks — BANNED. Use CSS Grid with explicit column definitions
- Floating/absolute-positioned decorative elements — BANNED
- Content overlapping other content causing z-index confusion — BANNED (compositional headline overlap at image boundary is fine)

### Responsive Strategy

**Breakpoints:**
- Desktop: `1440px` — full 12-column grid, maximum editorial composition
- Laptop: `1200px` — 12 columns, slightly compressed gutters
- Tablet: `768px` — 8-column grid, simplified two-column layouts
- Mobile: `390px` — single column, stacked, reordered for football-first priority

**Mobile is NOT a scaled-down desktop.** It is intentionally redesigned. Mobile content priority:
1. Next match / live match
2. Latest result
3. Upcoming fixtures
4. News headlines
5. Squad highlights
6. League standings

**Mobile-specific rules:**
- All multi-column layouts collapse to single column below `768px`
- Horizontal swipe carousels ONLY for: squad cards, fixture weeks, news categories
- Bottom-anchored CTA in ticket purchase flow
- Simplified hamburger menu — no bottom tab bar + hamburger simultaneously
- Section spacing reduces to `48px`
- No horizontal scroll on body — contained overflow ONLY within designated swipe components
- Typography scales: `clamp(1.75rem, 5vw, 3.5rem)` for headlines. Body stays `1rem` minimum
- Touch targets minimum `44px` all interactive elements
- Images: `max-width: 100%`, `object-fit: cover`

## 6. Surface & Texture

### Surface Treatment

ALL surfaces are **matte and opaque**. This is the defining visual quality. Every surface looks like it was painted, printed, or built — not rendered by a graphics engine.

- **Light surfaces:** Off White `#F3F3EF` or Pure White `#FFFFFF`. Flat, solid, zero gradient
- **Dark surfaces:** Dark Navy `#071C2C` or Charcoal `#17232C`. Flat, solid, zero gradient
- **Section transitions:** Hard cut between light and dark sections. Use `1px` border or `48–80px` spacing. NEVER gradient-fade between sections

### Physical Texture — Subtle, Atmospheric

When a section needs atmosphere beyond flat color, apply physical texture at LOW opacity via `::after` pseudo-element with `pointer-events: none` and `mix-blend-mode: overlay`:

- **Film grain:** CSS noise at `3–5%` opacity on dark hero sections, loading screen. Creates broadcast / documentary feel
- **Paper grain:** Noise at `2–3%` on Off White reading surfaces. Suggests printed programme
- **Concrete / stadium wall:** Photo texture at `5–8%` on heritage/history sections
- **Grass:** Faint grass texture at `3–5%` on match-related sections, only if contextually appropriate

**Texture rules:**
- Never exceed `8%` opacity
- Never make the site look dirty, aged, distressed, or vintage
- Textures are atmospheric — felt, not seen
- The site must still read as modern and clean
- No animated or moving textures

### Image Treatment — Documentary Photography

Photography is the primary source of visual drama. It must feel **captured, not constructed.**

**What to show:**
- Thiên Trường Stadium — stands, pitch, floodlights, tunnel
- Live match action — tackles, celebrations, corners, free kicks
- Supporters — flags, tifo, drums, scarves, chanting sections
- Players — training, warm-up, dressing room, post-match
- Historic moments — black-and-white archival, old programme covers
- Stadium atmosphere — rain, floodlit night, packed stands

**Color treatment:**
- Normal contrast, natural color
- Slight desaturation (`filter: saturate(0.9)`) for consistency across mixed-source photography
- NEVER artificially tint photos blue or add color wash
- Heritage images: `grayscale(0.8)` to `grayscale(1)` with optional `2–3%` grain

**Overlays:**
- Dark overlay ONLY when text must sit on photo for readability
- Overlay gradient: `linear-gradient(to top, rgba(7,28,44,0.85), rgba(7,28,44,0.3))` — Dark Navy based, NEVER pure black
- No colored overlays, no blue tint overlays

**Player imagery:**
- Natural lighting from the photograph
- No artificial blue rim lighting added post-capture
- No glow, no halo, no reflection on surface beneath
- No stylized cutout effects

**Aspect ratios:**
- Hero/banner: `16:9` or `21:9`
- News thumbnails: `3:2`
- Player portraits: `3:4`
- Stadium panoramas: `21:9` or `32:9`

**Image bans:**
- Artificial blue/neon lighting on photos — BANNED
- Glossy reflections beneath cutout images — BANNED
- Abstract AI-generated background shapes or blobs — BANNED
- Stock photography when club-specific imagery exists — BANNED
- Overly saturated or HDR-processed photos — BANNED

## 7. Motion & Interaction

### Motion Philosophy

Animation feels **mechanical and weighted** — like stadium gates closing, scoreboard digits flipping, floodlight rigging swinging. Not springy, not bouncy, not playful.

### Timing & Easing

- **Default easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)` — smooth deceleration, no elastic overshoot
- **Gate/panel easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` — heavy, deliberate, for loading screen panels
- **Enter:** `300ms ease-out`
- **Exit:** `200ms ease-in`
- **Hover:** `150ms` — immediate, responsive, no delay
- **No spring physics.** No bounce. No overshoot. Movement has finality. Things stop where they land

### Permitted Animations

- **Page transitions:** Simple fade or slide, `300ms`. No elaborate multi-step choreography
- **Scroll reveals:** Fade in + `translateY(16px)` on viewport entry. Stagger siblings by `50ms`. One pass — no re-trigger on scroll-up
- **Hover:** Color change, border darken, `translateY(-1px)`. All `150ms`. No scale
- **Loading screen gates:** `translateY` with gate easing at `800ms` (see Section 9)
- **Number counters:** Count-up on scroll-enter via `requestAnimationFrame`, `1200ms ease-out`
- **Tab content:** Height transition `250ms ease-out`, or simple show/hide
- **LIVE badge dot:** Small pulsing dot, the ONE permitted perpetual animation

### Banned Motion

- Spring physics (stiffness/damping parameters) — BANNED
- Parallax scrolling — BANNED
- Perpetual pulsing/floating/breathing loops (except LIVE dot) — BANNED
- 3D transforms / perspective rotations — BANNED
- Page morph transitions — BANNED
- Bouncing scroll chevrons / "scroll to explore" — BANNED
- Animated gradient backgrounds — BANNED
- Mouse-follow effects / custom cursors — BANNED
- Scale-on-hover above `1.02` — BANNED
- Lottie/Rive decorative animations — BANNED unless integral to content

### Performance

- Animate ONLY `transform` and `opacity`
- Never animate `width`, `height`, `top`, `left`, `margin`, `padding`
- Texture overlays on `position: fixed` pseudo-elements to avoid repaints
- `will-change` only on elements that actually animate, removed after animation completes

## 8. Shadows & Elevation

### Philosophy

The site is **flat**. Content hierarchy comes from background contrast, typography scale, and spatial position — not from floating layers or depth simulation. Things sit ON the page. Nothing hovers above it.

### Permitted Shadows (use sparingly)

- **Card (when necessary):** `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` — barely visible, almost none
- **Dropdown/popover:** `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` — functional overlay indication
- **Modal:** `0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)` — clear page separation

### Banned Shadows & Elevation

- Large soft drop shadows (`0 20px 60px ...`) — BANNED
- Colored shadows / blue glow / any tinted shadow — BANNED
- Inset shadows simulating depth — BANNED
- Multiple stacked shadow layers — BANNED
- `box-shadow` used as a glow or halo — BANNED
- `backdrop-filter: blur()` — BANNED everywhere
- `filter: drop-shadow()` with large values — BANNED
- Any visual that makes elements appear to float above the page — BANNED

## 9. Page-Specific Design Notes

### Loading Screen

**Concept:** Heavy stadium gates closing and opening — mechanical, anticipatory, physical.

- Viewport splits horizontally: top panel and bottom panel
- Both panels: solid Dark Navy `#071C2C`, film grain at `3%` opacity
- Faint Thiên Trường Stadium photograph visible through grain at `8–12%` opacity
- Panels slide toward center: top down, bottom up. Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` at `800ms`
- When panels meet: official club crest appears center, clean and crisp — NO glow, NO shine, NO neon outline, NO pulsing
- 3 championship stars render in Gold `#C3A044` above crest — the only gold on this screen
- Thin progress line: `2px` height, Club Blue `#009DDA`, filling left to right at panel meeting point
- On 100%: panels separate, reveal homepage
- Total duration: `2.5–3s`
- The feeling: heavy steel stadium gates closing before kickoff, then opening to reveal the pitch. NOT a futuristic loading screen

### Homepage

**Feel:** Front page of the official club publication.

- **Hero:** Full-bleed Thiên Trường photography. Strong `Bebas Neue` headline — match announcement, season message, or club news. Dark Navy overlay ONLY for text readability. ONE primary CTA button. No secondary links. No "scroll to explore." The photo creates the drama
- **Match Center:** Flat Dark Navy section. Resembles a physical scoreboard or stadium LED display. `Bebas Neue` scores at large scale. `JetBrains Mono` for times. `1px rgba(255,255,255,0.1)` dividers. Team crests at actual size, clean rendering. Live/upcoming/result via badge system. NOT a glossy card
- **News Section:** Editorial magazine layout. One large feature (7 columns) beside 2–3 smaller stories (5 columns). Varied image sizes. NOT a uniform 3-card grid. Strong photography. Thin dividers between items
- **Squad Preview:** Matchday-poster composition on Dark Navy. Natural player cutouts. Large `Bebas Neue` jersey numbers (`6–10rem`) partially behind players as graphic elements. Name in `Archivo Condensed 700`. No card wrapping, no glow, no reflection beneath players, no trading-card effect
- **Heritage Section:** Desaturated/black-and-white photography. Championship years `1965`, `1984`, `1985` as large `Bebas Neue` graphic numbers. Gold stars — ONLY gold usage. Paper or concrete texture at low opacity optional. Documentary feeling
- **Thiên Trường Section:** Panoramic stadium photography dominates. Minimal text overlay. Let the image be the content. No blueprint wireframe, no futuristic glow, no 3D model

### Squad List Page

- Grid: 4 columns desktop, 2 columns tablet, 2 columns mobile (or horizontal swipe)
- Player: natural photo, `Bebas Neue` jersey number large, `Archivo Condensed 700` name, `Be Vietnam Pro 400` position small
- Group by position: Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, Forwards
- Position headers: `Bebas Neue` uppercase + horizontal rule below
- Background blocks per group (alternating Off White / White or Off White / Light Navy). INSTEAD of individual card borders
- If cards: `6px` radius, `1px` border, zero shadow. No shine, no metallic, no holographic, no trading-card effects
- Feels like: official team sheet pinned to dressing room wall

### Player Detail Page

- Large player photograph: minimum 50% viewport width on desktop, full width on mobile
- Oversized jersey number: `Bebas Neue` at `8–12rem`, positioned as background graphic behind or beside photo. Semi-transparent (`15–25%` opacity) or compositionally layered
- Player name: `Archivo Black` at hero scale
- Stats: typographic grid with large `Bebas Neue` numbers + `Be Vietnam Pro 400` labels. Separated by thin `1px` rules. NOT stat cards, NOT radial charts, NOT progress bars, NOT a dashboard
- Bio: reading-width column (`65ch`), `Be Vietnam Pro 400`, `1.65` line height
- Feels like: magazine player profile spread

### News List Page

- Magazine layout: dominant feature story at top (full width or asymmetric split), then varied-size entries
- Category navigation: horizontal `Archivo Condensed 600`, NO pills, active = Club Blue text + `2px` bottom border, inactive = `#5C6970`
- Article entries: image + headline + date + category. Varied sizes — mix at least 2 sizes per row
- NOT an identical card grid. Photography-driven — every article shows its image prominently
- Feels like: FourFourTwo magazine contents page

### News Detail Page

- Reading-optimized: `760–820px` body width, centered
- Headline: `Archivo Black` or `Bebas Neue` at display scale
- Meta: date + category + author in `Be Vietnam Pro 400` at `0.875rem`, `#5C6970`
- Hero image: full body width, `3:2` or `16:9`
- Body: `Be Vietnam Pro 400`, `1rem`, `1.65` line height, `#1A2530`
- Pull quotes: `Archivo Condensed 600 Italic`, `1.5rem`, left border `3px` Club Blue
- Minimal UI chrome — content IS the interface
- Feels like: longform sports journalism article

### Competitions Page

- Selector: horizontal tabs `Archivo Condensed 600` on desktop, dropdown on mobile
- Active tab: `2px` bottom border Club Blue. No pill backgrounds, no filled tabs
- Standings table: clean alternating rows, strong typographic hierarchy
- Team logos small and consistent. Names in `Archivo Condensed 600`
- Position numbers in `Bebas Neue`. Points/GD in `JetBrains Mono`
- Feels like: league table in a newspaper

### Fixtures Page

- Grouped by month: large `Bebas Neue` month/year headers
- Match rows: horizontal — date, home crest + name, score/time, away crest + name, venue, status badge
- NOT individual cards. Use `1px` dividers between rows
- Upcoming: `JetBrains Mono` time, secondary text color
- Live: `#D42B2B` LIVE badge + pulsing dot, score in `Bebas Neue`
- Finished: FT badge, final score, subtle W/D/L color indicator
- Mobile: simplified rows, swipe between months
- Feels like: match schedule page in a programme

### Ticket Page

- Matchday service feel, NOT e-commerce
- Step indicator: numbered `1–4` in `Archivo Condensed 600`. Active = Club Blue. Completed = Dark Navy. Upcoming = Light Gray. Simple, linear, no fancy stepper
- Stadium plan: flat SVG diagram of Thiên Trường. Stand sections as clickable regions with background color hover. NO 3D rendering, NO perspective view, NO glass overlay
- Pricing: `Bebas Neue` numbers, clear and large
- CTA: anchored visibly, solid Club Blue button
- Steps: 1. Match → 2. Stand → 3. Quantity → 4. Payment
- Feels like: ticket counter at the stadium, not an airline booking engine

### Mobile Versions

- NOT scaled-down desktop. Intentionally redesigned for mobile context and priority
- Navigation: hamburger menu OR bottom nav — never both simultaneously
- Next match widget always accessible — football information first
- Horizontal swipe: fixtures by week, squad by position, news by category
- Simplified decorative elements: fewer textures, simpler transitions
- All touch targets: `44px` minimum
- No horizontal body scroll under any circumstance
- Feels like: checking scores and club info on the bus to the stadium

## 10. Anti-Patterns — Strictly Banned

This checklist exists because AI tools produce these patterns by default. Every item must be actively avoided.

### Visual Effects — BANNED
- Glassmorphism / frosted glass / `backdrop-filter: blur()`
- Glossy cards / glossy surfaces / reflective surfaces
- Neon glow / blue glow / any `box-shadow` used as a glow
- Glowing logos / glowing borders / glowing text
- Large soft drop shadows (`> 4px` blur, `> 0.08` opacity)
- Liquid gradients / animated gradients / mesh gradients
- Fake 3D surfaces / metallic / chrome / holographic effects
- Artificial rim lighting on player photos
- Abstract AI background shapes / decorative blobs / wave patterns
- Excessive transparency / layered translucent panels
- Cyberpunk / esports / HUD visual language
- Lens flare or light leak effects

### Layout — BANNED
- 3-column equal card grids (use varied sizes)
- Every content block wrapped in a rounded card (use editorial layout)
- Pill-shaped components everywhere (`border-radius: 40px+`)
- Rounded corners above `10px` on any element
- Centered hero layout when asymmetric would be stronger
- "Bento grid" trendy layouts
- Masonry/Pinterest layout for non-image content

### Typography — BANNED
- Inter font (AI default)
- Poppins font (AI default)
- Generic serif fonts (Times, Georgia, Garamond)
- Every text element in uppercase (reserve for display only)
- Gradient text fills
- Text shadows / text glow / text outlines
- "Scroll to explore" / scroll arrows / bouncing chevrons
- Thin/light font weights at display sizes

### Motion — BANNED
- Spring physics / bouncy animations
- Parallax scrolling
- Perpetual pulsing/floating micro-interactions (except LIVE dot)
- Scale-on-hover above `1.02`
- Custom mouse cursors / cursor effects
- Animated gradient backgrounds
- Mouse-follow / magnetic hover effects
- Lottie/Rive decorative animations

### Content — BANNED
- AI copywriting clichés: "Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen", "Cutting-edge", "Redefine", "Transform", "Revolutionary"
- Fabricated statistics or metrics — never invent numbers
- Generic placeholder names ("John Doe", "Acme")
- Emojis in UI
- `SYSTEM // 2024` label formatting
- Fake round numbers (`99.99%`, `50%`)
- "BY THE NUMBERS" dashboard sections with invented data
- English placeholder text when Vietnamese is required

### Photography — BANNED
- Artificially blue-tinted photos
- Neon lighting added to player images in post-processing
- Glossy reflections beneath player cutouts
- AI-generated abstract backgrounds
- Stock photography when club imagery would work
- Oversaturated HDR processing
